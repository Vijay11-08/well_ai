# Product Requirements Document (PRD): NeuroNiv

## 1. Overview
NeuroNiv is an AI-Native Product Engineering platform. The primary goal of the NeuroNiv landing page is to attract startups, growing businesses, and enterprise teams that require reliable engineering and AI-native software delivery. The design will emphasize a premium, cutting-edge aesthetic with high interactivity to reflect the advanced nature of the services offered.

## 2. Target Audience
- Founders and Product Teams
- Startups and Scale-ups needing technical execution
- Enterprises requiring modernization and AI integration

## 3. Core Features
- **Hero Section:** High-impact value proposition with dynamic text, gradient accents, and a primary Call to Action (CTA).
- **Social Proof:** Auto-playing video/animated cards highlighting industry experience, cloud-native delivery, and AI capabilities.
- **Partner Logos:** A continuous marquee scrolling list of integrated technologies and partner teams (e.g., AWS, Azure, GCP).
- **Service Highlights:** Clear comparison of the "NeuroNiv Way" (AI-Native Engineering) versus traditional development, featuring glassmorphic cards and glowing borders.

## 4. Non-Functional Requirements
- **Performance:** Optimized loading times; CSS and JS animations must be hardware-accelerated.
- **Responsiveness:** Fully mobile-responsive utilizing Bootstrap's grid system.
- **Aesthetics:** Dark theme, hand-drawn SVG highlights, deep purples/pinks, and a custom interactive cursor.

## 5. Success Criteria
- The landing page accurately conveys a premium brand image.
- High engagement measured by scroll depth and CTA clicks.
- Smooth performance on both desktop and mobile devices without animation stuttering.
